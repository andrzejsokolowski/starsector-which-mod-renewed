[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Only What the Game Does Not Show[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]With Random Assortment of Things, the [b]F2 codex[/b] link at the bottom of many tooltips names the mod. Those tooltips stay as they are.[/li]
[li]Every other tooltip gets one line at the bottom: the codex mod icon and [b]Mod: name[/b], or [b]Mod: Vanilla[/b].[/li]
[li]It works for things from every mod, with no list to keep up to date.[/li]
[li]Ships, fighters, weapons, ship systems, hull mods, special items, blueprints, commodities, industries, conditions, skills, abilities and terrain.[/li]
[li]Blueprints and modspecs name the mod of what they unlock.[/li]
[li]Star systems on the sector map say which mod made them. Generated systems show nothing.[/li]
[li]zzz BlockTags and Animated Colony Items only change vanilla things, so those things show as Vanilla, also in the codex.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Make It Yours (LunaLib, optional)[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]One switch per kind:[/b] ships and fighters, weapons, ship systems, hull mods, special items and blueprints, commodities, industries, conditions, skills, abilities, terrain, star systems, other descriptions.[/li]
[li][b]Not where the codex link names the mod:[/b] on by default. Turn it off to see the line in every tooltip.[/li]
[li][b]Treat as Vanilla:[/b] the mods whose changes count as Vanilla. Add any mod that only edits vanilla things.[/li]
[li]Changes apply right away, no restart.[/li]
[li]Without LunaLib everything is on, with the defaults above.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Needs PatchLib.[/li]
[li]Turn off WhichMod and Which-Industry, or those tooltips show the mod twice.[/li]
[li]Safe to add to or remove from a game in progress. It only notes in each new star system which mod made it.[/li]
[/list]
