[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Every tooltip says which mod the thing comes from, or Vanilla.
[/b][/center]
[center]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]The Line[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]At the bottom of the tooltip: the codex mod icon and "Mod: name", or "Mod: Vanilla" for things from the base game.[/li]
[li]The name is the one the codex shows for that entry.[/li]
[li]Ships, fighters, weapons, hull mods and ship systems.[/li]
[li]Special items, including items whose tooltip never shows their description.[/li]
[li]Blueprints and modspecs name the mod of the ship, weapon, fighter, hull mod or industry they unlock.[/li]
[li]Commodities, industries and structures, planet conditions.[/li]
[li]Skills, abilities and terrain on the campaign map.[/li]
[li]The codex is left as it is, it already shows the mod.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Needs PatchLib.[/li]
[li]Turn off WhichMod and Which-Industry, or those tooltips show the mod twice.[/li]
[li]Saves nothing into your game: safe to add to or remove from a game in progress.[/li]
[/list]
