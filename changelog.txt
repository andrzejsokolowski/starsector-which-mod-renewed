Version 1.1.0 (2026-11-16)
- The line shows only in tooltips without the F2 codex link, which already names the mod. A setting turns this off.
- zzz BlockTags and Animated Colony Items show as Vanilla, also in the codex. The list is a setting.
- Star systems on the sector map say which mod made them. Generated systems show nothing.
- Optional LunaLib settings: turn the line on or off for each kind of tooltip.

Version 1.0.1 (2026-11-16)
- Every tooltip says which mod the thing comes from, or Vanilla: ships, fighters, weapons, hull mods, ship systems, special items, blueprints, commodities, industries, conditions, skills, abilities and terrain.
- The line sits at the bottom of the tooltip, with the codex mod icon.
- Blueprints and modspecs name the mod of the ship, weapon, fighter, hull mod or industry they unlock.
