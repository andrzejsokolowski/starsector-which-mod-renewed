Version 2.0.1 (2026-11-17)
- The line now shows in tooltips whose F2 codex link does not name the mod, such as vanilla things, ship systems and weapons Random Assortment of Things does not label. Before, any codex link hid it.
- Without Random Assortment of Things the line shows in every tooltip, since the game's codex link never names the mod.

Version 2.0.0 (2026-11-16)
- First release on the forum. Numbered 2.0.0 so mod managers offer it over the 1.1.0 test build.
- Says which mod a thing comes from, or Vanilla, only where the game does not show it already: tooltips without the F2 codex link, which names the mod.
- Covers ships, fighters, weapons, ship systems, hull mods, special items, blueprints, commodities, industries, conditions, skills, abilities, terrain and star systems on the sector map.
- Blueprints and modspecs name the mod of what they unlock. Generated star systems show nothing.
- zzz BlockTags and Animated Colony Items show as Vanilla, also in the codex.
- Optional LunaLib settings: one switch per kind, show the line next to codex links too, and the list of mods shown as Vanilla.
